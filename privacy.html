Privacy policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

anonshare is a live, end-to-end encrypted scratchpad. It has no accounts and sets no cookies. This page describes precisely what happens to your data, including the parts that are less flattering.

What is never collected

  • No account, email address, or phone number.
  • No cookies, and no advertising or third-party tracking scripts of any kind.
  • No tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cross-site profiling.
  • No profile of you, and nothing sold or shared with anyone.

Cookies

anonshare sets zero cookies, on the marketing site or inside a room. Whatever a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal your browser sends, the answer is already the same: nothing is tracked either way, because there is no cookie or fingerprinting mechanism to switch off in the first place.

What you type

Everything in a room - the text, the file names, the chat, and your display name - is encrypted in your browser with AES-GCM before it is sent anywhere. The key is derived from the room code and the room password using PBKDF2, and it never leaves your device.

The relay server stores and forwards sealed bytes it cannot read. It has no key, so it cannot decrypt your content, and neither can I.

Important: anyone who has the room code, and the password if one is set, can read the room. Encryption protects the content in transit and at rest on the relay. It cannot protect you from someone you handed the code to.

What the relay unavoidably sees

To route messages at all, the relay necessarily processes some metadata:

  • The room code, and whether that room has a password.
  • A hash of the room password token and of the owner token. The tokens themselves are not stored.
  • The size and timing of encrypted messages, and how many people are connected.
  • Your IP address, transiently, as part of the network connection and to rate-limit abuse. It is not written into any room record or long-term log.

How long anything is kept

A room and everything in it is destroyed when it expires. You choose the lifetime when you create it: 10 minutes, 1 hour, or 24 hours after everyone has left. The owner can also delete a room immediately, which erases it from the relay for everybody.

What stays on your own device

anonshare stores a few things locally in your browser, not on any server:

  • localStorage: your display name, colour, theme, chosen relay, and an owner token for any room you created.
  • IndexedDB: an offline copy of rooms you have opened, so you can keep working without a connection.

You can erase these at any time with Settings → Delete offline copy, or by clearing site data in your browser. Losing the owner token means losing owner control of that room; it cannot be recovered or reissued.

Site analytics

The marketing site (not the room itself) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to see basic, aggregate traffic like page views and load performance. It is deliberately privacy-first: it sets no cookies, does no fingerprinting, and cannot identify or follow you across sites. It cannot see anything typed inside a room, since room traffic never touches it.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, runs the relay, and provides the cookieless analytics above, including standard network-level logs and abuse protection.
  • esm.sh serves the editor JavaScript modules. Loading the page makes a request to that CDN, which means it sees your IP address, as any CDN would.

No other third party receives anything. The site makes no calls to social networks, code hosts, font services, or advertising networks.

International data transfers

anonshare runs on Cloudflare's global network, so requests and encrypted traffic may pass through data centres outside your own country in the ordinary course of routing. Cloudflare's own data-processing terms govern that infrastructure layer. There is no separate regional copy of anything to transfer, because rooms are not stored anywhere once they expire.

Legal requests

If law enforcement or a court ever compels disclosure, I can only hand over what the relay actually holds: the room code, whether the room is password protected, hashed tokens (never the tokens themselves), and connection metadata such as IP addresses and timing. I cannot hand over room content, because the relay never has the key to decrypt it. Where I am not legally prohibited from doing so, I will disclose that a request was made and what was provided.

Children

anonshare is not directed at children under 13, and no age information is collected because no personal information is collected.

Your rights

Because no account exists and content is encrypted and short-lived, there is generally no personal data held about you that could be exported or deleted on request. To the extent a jurisdiction's law (such as the GDPR or CCPA) would otherwise give you rights of access, correction, deletion, or objection, they are satisfied in practice like this:

  • Access / export: there is nothing on the relay to export; anything you wrote lives in the room itself, which you already have access to while it exists.
  • Deletion: delete the room (as its owner) to erase it immediately, or simply let it expire.
  • Correction: edit the room directly, since it is a live document.
  • Objection to processing: stop using the service; there is no persistent profile to object to.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be reflected on the site itself rather than announced by email, since there is no email list.
